# Third-party assets

This public repository contains the original gameplay code, editor tooling,
scene configuration, project settings, and documentation for *The Littles:
Edmonton School Run*.

Raw third-party art packages are not redistributed here. They include licensed
Unity Asset Store or creator-provided packages used in the compiled game, such
as low-poly city, street, vegetation, character, and animation assets.

Those packages remain subject to their original authors' licences and are not
covered by this repository's MIT License. Downloadable release builds contain
only the compiled assets needed to play the game.
